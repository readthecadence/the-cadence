## VII. THE CREED

> **1** I believe in the Objective, worthy and unmeasurable, and in its Key Results, by which it is made known.
> **2** I believe the measure is the evidence and not the thing.
> **3** I hold the honest 0.7 above the timid 1.0.
> **4** I keep the Cadence, I ladder to the whole, and I confess my numbers as they are.
> **5** I watch, for I know not the hour of the Inflection.
