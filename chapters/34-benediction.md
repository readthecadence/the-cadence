> May you aim past your grasp and confess your grades without fear.
> May you never report the watermelon.
> May you see your Inflection while there is still time to turn.
> May you build the thing right, and know that it was the right thing to build.
> And may you wield the tireless hand, yet keep company with the living, in the living voice.*
