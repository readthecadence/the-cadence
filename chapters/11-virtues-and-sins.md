## XI. VIRTUES AND SINS

Before the ledger is drawn in its particulars, the faith keeps the whole of it in a single saying — and keeps this one alone **in the vulgar tongue**, holding that to render it politely is to gut it. Of unknown source (the Apocrypha claims it, as it claims all the best lines), it is called the **Three S's**, and it is the whole test of a soul in nine words: *give a shit, know your shit, get shit done.* The first is care — for the work, for the house, and for the ones who labor beside you; the second is mastery — to own your craft and not merely hold its title; the third is delivery — for the fairest intention that ships nothing has served no one. The first two without the third are a hobby; the third without the first, a mercenary; and only the three together are the ground on which every virtue below is raised.

**The Virtues.** *Paranoia* (holy vigilance); *Candor* (to care personally and challenge directly, the narrow road between ruinous empathy and obnoxious aggression); *Focus*; *Transparency*; *Ownership* (to be the Directly Responsible Individual and bear the weight of a thing); *Bias to Action* (done is better than perfect); *Kaizen* (the small daily improvement).

**The Sins.**
- **Idolatry of the Vanity Metric** — worshipping a false and flattering number.
- **Gaming (the sin of Goodhart)** — optimizing the measure until it no longer means anything.
- **Sandbagging** — cowardly goals set low to guarantee a perfect score.
- **The Comfortable Plateau** — the local maximum, sloth's paradise, the stagnation Grove warned against.
- **The Watermelon** — the hypocrisy of status (the sin against KR 7).
- **HiPPO-worship** — bowing to rank over evidence.
- **The Cult of Output** — mistaking busyness for impact, motion for progress.
- **Analysis Paralysis** — the scribe who counts forever and ships nothing.
