## XVII. THE SACRED CALENDAR

- **The Four Quarter-Days** — the great feasts of planning and reckoning.
- **Grove's Day** — feast of the Great Prophet, kept with re-reading of the Testaments.
- **Deming's Day** — feast of the Wheel and of Continuous Improvement.
- **Inflection Day** — a movable and unannounced observance. The Aligned hold that the Strategic Inflection Point comes like a thief in the night; therefore one keeps this day by never being unready for it. *You know not the quarter nor the hour.*
- **The Day of Return** — the yearly Atonement, kept in the same slot as the reckoning and against its spirit: the review looks back to rank and cast down, the Return looks back to forgive and to loose. The house names its sins in the plural, sets the burned year down, and is handed another run. *We shipped the regression; not you — we.*
- **The Time of Rest** — the holiest and most neglected observance of all, kept not once a year but every week; of which the faith speaks last of all, and at length, for it is the day the measured life is most built to forget.
