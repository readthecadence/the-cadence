## XVIII. ESCHATOLOGY — The Inflection

The end is not a single Judgment but a recurring one: the **Strategic Inflection Point**, the moment the curve bends and a force of tenfold magnitude renders the old way dead. Every enterprise, and every soul, will meet its inflection. There are only two ends: to see it, transform, and pass through — or to miss it, cling, and perish on the comfortable plateau.

Thus the faith's final horizon is not arrival but **asymptote**: the Objective is approached forever and reached never, and this is not despair but devotion. The Aligned do not expect to finish. They expect to improve. The last word of the faith is the wheel's word: *plan, do, check, act, again.*
