# On the Submission of Heresies

The faith is not finished and does not pretend to be. Every schism in its
history began as one person saying *that is not quite right*, and most of
them were correct.

## How to petition

Open an Issue. That is the whole mechanism.

Pull requests are closed, and will stay closed. The reason is not pride: a
scripture written by committee is a style guide, and the register is the
delivery system for everything else. Your idea is wanted. Your prose, with
respect and affection, is not.

## What makes a good demon

The Bestiary does not catalogue mistakes. It catalogues **the mistakes that
arrive dressed as virtues** — that is the whole form. A submission needs
three things:

1. **A name**, and the plain name beneath it. (*The Wraith of the Long Road*
   is the plain *Sunk Cost*.)
2. **The disguise** — the virtue it wears when it walks in. If it does not
   look like a good idea at the time, it is not a demon, it is just a bad
   decision.
3. **A ward** — the one question that breaks the spell. A demon with no ward
   is a complaint.

## What makes a good parable

It must be **true**, it must carry **one** doctrine, and it must not explain
itself in the last line. If it needs a moral appended, the story has not done
its work.

## Putting a question to the faith

Ask about a **system**, not about your life. *We are eighteen months into a
rewrite and cannot kill it* is a question the canon can answer. *Should I quit
my job* is not, and the faith will decline it — kindly, and in character.

Every answer names its verse. If the canon is silent, you will be told that it
is silent, which is a real answer and the only honest one available.

## The register

Straight-faced or not at all. No winking, no "lol," no breaking character to
signal that you are in on it. The joke is that nobody is joking.

---

## The unglamorous part, said plainly

Everything above is in character. This paragraph is not.

By opening an Issue you are offering an idea to the project and agreeing that
it may be used, adapted, and published — including in a book that may
eventually be sold — without payment or obligation. You keep the right to
your own idea; you are not signing it away, and you are not being hired.
Credit for anything substantial will be given in the acknowledgements unless
you ask us not to.

If that is not acceptable to you, please do not open the Issue. No hard
feelings, and thank you for reading this far.
